About

I build the operational layer that e-commerce brands run on — storefronts, fulfillment logic, and the automations that connect them, so the team spends less time on repetitive tasks.

Most of my day lives inside Shopify and the tools that talk to it: Flow for the logic, Klaviyo for the follow-up, Gorgias for support, DSers for fulfillment, and Slack to keep everyone in the loop. I lean on Claude for the parts of the job that benefit from a second set of hands — drafting, debugging, and thinking through edge cases.

If a task is repeated by hand more than a couple of times, my instinct is to build it once and let it run.
